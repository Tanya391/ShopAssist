# Express Shipping & Delivery Policy

## 1. Shipping Methods & Carriers
ShopAssist Store partners with tier-1 global carriers including **FedEx**, **UPS**, **DHL Express**, and **USPS** to ensure fast and secure parcel delivery.

---

## 2. Delivery Rates & Estimated Timelines

| Shipping Tier | Estimated Transit Time | Cost | Order Threshold |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard Ground** | 3 – 5 Business Days | $4.99 | Free on orders over $50 |
| **Expedited Priority** | 2 Business Days | $12.99 | Flat rate |
| **Overnight Express** | 1 Business Day | $24.99 | Orders placed before 2 PM EST |

> **Warehouse Order Processing:** Orders placed before **2:00 PM EST (Monday–Friday)** are processed and dispatched on the same business day. Orders placed on weekends or national holidays are dispatched on the next business day.

---

## 3. Order Tracking & Status Monitoring
Upon fulfillment, customers receive a shipping confirmation email and SMS containing a carrier-verified tracking number (e.g. `FX-99882211`).

### Live Tracking Status Definitions:
- **Processing:** Order validated; warehouse packing in progress.
- **Shipped:** Package handed over to carrier; tracking active.
- **Out for Delivery:** Package on carrier vehicle for final delivery.
- **Delivered:** Successfully delivered to doorstep or parcel box.

---

## 4. Lost, Missing, or Delayed Shipments
If tracking shows no movement for more than **3 consecutive business days**, or if an order is marked "Delivered" but cannot be located:
1. Check around parcel boxes, side doors, and with neighbors.
2. Contact ShopAssist AI chat with your Order ID (e.g., `ORD-1002`) to immediately check carrier telemetry.
3. If unlocated after 24 hours, our AI assistant automatically registers a **Missing Package Support Ticket** for immediate replacement dispatch.
