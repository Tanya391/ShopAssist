# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## 1. General Ordering & Account Questions

### Q: Can I modify or cancel my order after placing it?
**A:** You can modify or cancel your order within **30 minutes** of placing it. After 30 minutes, the order enters automated fulfillment at our warehouse and cannot be canceled, but you may return it upon arrival for a full refund.

### Q: What payment methods do you accept?
**A:** We accept Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and ShopAssist Store Gift Cards.

---

## 2. Order Tracking & Delivery FAQs

### Q: How do I check where my package is right now?
**A:** Simply type your Order ID (for example: `ORD-1001`, `ORD-1002`, or `ORD-1003`) into the ShopAssist AI chat window. The bot directly accesses carrier databases to provide your delivery date, carrier status, and tracking link.

### Q: What should I do if my package says "Delivered" but I haven't received it?
**A:** Please allow up to 24 hours as carriers sometimes mark parcels delivered slightly ahead of physical drop-off. If still missing, notify ShopAssist AI in chat and a support ticket will be immediately escalated.

---

## 3. Product & Warranty FAQs

### Q: Do products come with a manufacturer warranty?
**A:** Yes! All new electronics and tech hardware include a **1-Year Manufacturer Warranty** covering mechanical and electronic failure. Extended protection plans (2-Year and 3-Year accidental damage) can be added during checkout.
