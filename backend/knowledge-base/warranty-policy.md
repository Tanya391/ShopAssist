# Manufacturer Warranty & Product Guarantee Coverage

## 1. Limited Hardware Warranty Terms
ShopAssist guarantees all original consumer hardware products against defects in materials and workmanship under normal use for a period of **ONE (1) YEAR** from the date of retail purchase by the original end-user purchaser ("Warranty Period").

---

## 2. Warranty Coverage Comparison

| Coverage Type | Standard 1-Year Warranty | Extended Care+ Plan (3-Year) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Manufacturing Defects** | Covered (100%) | Covered (100%) |
| **Power Surge & Battery Failures** | Covered (100%) | Covered (100%) |
| **Accidental Drops & Liquid Damage** | Not Covered | Covered (Up to 2 claims/yr) |
| **Free Express Replacement Shipping** | Included | Included |

---

## 3. How to Submit a Warranty Claim
1. Contact ShopAssist AI in chat and specify your product model and serial number or original Order ID.
2. Provide a short description of the hardware malfunction (e.g. key press non-responsive, screen backlight failure).
3. The AI agent will run remote diagnostic steps or generate a **Warranty Replacement Ticket** for immediate warehouse RMA processing.
