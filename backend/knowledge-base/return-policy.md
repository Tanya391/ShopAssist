# Product Returns & Exchange Procedures

## 1. Simple 3-Step Return Process
Returning an item at ShopAssist is quick and automated:

1. **Initiate Request:** Contact ShopAssist AI in the chat or navigate to the Returns Center.
2. **Prepaid Shipping Label:** Download and print your prepaid carrier return shipping label (FedEx / USPS).
3. **Drop Off Parcel:** Attach label to box and drop off at any authorized shipping location.

---

## 2. Return Window Guidelines

| Category | Standard Return Window | Extended Holiday Window | Return Shipping Fee |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Consumer Electronics** | 30 Days from delivery | Nov 1 – Jan 15 (Up to Jan 31) | Free for defective/damaged |
| **Apparel & Accessories** | 45 Days from delivery | Same | Always Free |
| **Clearance / Open Box** | 14 Days from delivery | N/A | $5.99 Deduction |

---

## 3. Damaged or Defective Items
If your item arrives damaged, cracked, or malfunctioning:
- **Do not send it back without reporting.**
- Provide your Order ID to ShopAssist AI chat (e.g. `ORD-1003`).
- The AI assistant will issue a **Priority Replacement Order** and automatically create a **Damaged Product Support Ticket**.
- A prepaid return shipping label will be e-mailed to you with no cost to you.
